## Qualified conflicts and exclusions

- [DR08](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR08): A 50-test floor might permit a documented override. **Disposition:** Not adopted as permission. The active v3 requirement remains at least 50 meaningful module-owned cases; no filler and no silent override.
- [DR12](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR12): Historic start-coding gates, mining and cockpit re-entry commands. **Disposition:** Historical HEE-v2 instructions are not v3 authorization. No mining, respawn, service activation or runtime implementation is performed from note commands.
- [DR13](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR13): Use :z rather than :Z as a universal fix for read-only mounts. **Disposition:** Not adopted. Podman documents ro/rw separately from z/Z; z requests shared SELinux labeling and Z private labeling. Relabeling depends on host permissions and context. No automatic relabel or SELinux disable is prescribed. [Primary documentation](https://docs.podman.io/en/latest/markdown/podman-run.1.html).
- [DR13](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR13): Rootless containers remove security boundaries and read-only sources make contamination impossible. **Disposition:** Not adopted literally. Rootless mode and read-only mounts are scoped controls; shared outputs, credentials, network, user namespaces and host/container custody still require qualification. [Primary documentation](https://docs.podman.io/en/latest/markdown/podman.1.html).
- [DR07](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR07): Historic thematic percentages, correlations and a proposed future rerun. **Disposition:** Retained as historical interpretation. The reflection reports missing replay tooling; this update does not reproduce those metrics or infer causal improvement.
- [DR05](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR05): Inherited traits or identity imply reliable behavior. **Disposition:** No persona/identity is imported. Transfer only specific observed failure classes, triggers and falsifiable verification obligations.
- [DR03](obsidian://open?vault=herdr-engineering-engine-v3.vault&file=Learnings%2FSources%2FDR03): Blanket method bans, arbitrary antipattern numbering or mandatory self-criticism. **Disposition:** Use the local LRN namespace, consumer-specific invariants and measured evidence. Do not ban safe bounded forms or manufacture weaknesses.

## Existing mechanisms versus future proof

The corpus updater already checks exact projections, receipt subject hashes, reciprocal anchors and source-body preservation. This edition adds capture/lesson coverage, diary backlink verification and test-discovery receipt coverage. These mechanisms qualify documentation consistency only. The proposed engine detectors, ≥50-case module qualification, independent admission and released-migration freeze enforcement remain unavailable.

## Cost and retirement ledger

Track source/subject scope, added/changed files and helper lines, wall time and resource cost, actual triggered checks, known prevented failures, false positives and rework, remaining gaps, retirement/merge disposition. Use actual files and execution records, not a prior include list or a positive completion label. Document growth is measured as maintenance cost; it is not knowledge gained or a top-tail performance score. Do not infer a prevention rate before the engine executes meaningful workloads.
