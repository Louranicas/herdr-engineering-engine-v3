**Current test priorities:** ["schema/version compatibility", "identity/correlation integrity", "bounded decode and malformed inputs", "typed errors and serialization invariants"]. The governing standard requires at least 50 distinct meaningful primary-owned cases, zero baseline warnings/errors, assimilation and mutation evidence.
